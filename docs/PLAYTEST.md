# Playtest guide

Play for 15–30 minutes, ideally once on a phone and once on a computer. Then jot down short answers to whichever of these questions are useful. Copy the answers into the "Playtest notes" part of the current iteration in `DEVLOG.md`, so the next version can respond to them.

## Feeling
1. In a few words, how did it feel to be there?
2. Was there a moment you slowed down or lingered? Where?
3. Did anything feel rushed, busy, game-like or "gamified"?

## Walking & finding your way (v2: 3D)
4. Was tapping to walk comfortable? Did you ever get stuck, or walk somewhere you didn't mean to?
5. Could you tell the symbols apart (look / go / sit / talk / bell)? Did the Nearby buttons help?
6. Did the signposts help you find places? Did you get lost?
7. Which area felt most alive? Which felt flattest or emptiest?
8. Any motion discomfort? Did field of view, "turn to face where you walk" or reduced motion help?
9. Did it run smoothly on your phone? Which visual quality did you use?

## People, animals & the bell
10. Did the people and animals make the valley feel lived in? Too many, too few?
11. How did meeting Sister Hien feel?
12. When the bell sounded and everything paused, was it calming, or did it interrupt you? Was the frequency right?

## Photography
8. Did the camera make you notice things? What did you photograph?
9. Did framing, zooming and focusing feel natural (touch and mouse)?
10. Was developing film each evening pleasant, or annoying?
11. Did your photos look like what you framed?

## Story & meaning
12. What did you do with the broken bowl? What did you expect to happen?
13. Did anything feel preachy, confusing or culturally off?
14. Did sitting work for you? What happened with the thoughts?

## Sound & music
15. Did you hear the nature sounds change as you moved (stream, wind, frogs, rain)?
16. Was the music calming? Too much, too little, too repetitive?
17. Were the captions useful?

## Technical
17. Device / browser used, and did it run smoothly? (Settings → Visual quality)
18. Any bugs, stuck moments, or unreadable text?

## For next time
19. If you could change one thing, what would it be?
20. What would you like to discover next?
